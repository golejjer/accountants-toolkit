# Examplia general statutory pack: guidance

Examplia is a fictional country used to show the pack format. Nothing here describes a real jurisdiction.

## When this pack applies

A business established in Examplia. The platform switches taxes on from the business's answers: VAT if it is registered, the turnover levy if it is liable, and withholding on service fees for every business. The platform calculates every amount; never work one out yourself.

## Choosing a VAT code on a sale

- **Standard-rated (VAT-STD):** most sales of goods and services inside Examplia.
- **Zero-rated (VAT-ZERO):** exported goods, and services supplied to a customer outside Examplia and used outside it. Ask: "Where is the customer based, and where will the service be used?" Keep the export evidence (VAT Act Schedule 2, fictional).
- **Exempt (VAT-EX):** the exempt list in VAT Act Schedule 3 (fictional), for example certain financial and medical services. Ask what the service is before choosing it; exempt is not the same as zero-rated.

## Choosing a VAT code on a purchase

- **Standard-rated (VAT-STD):** the supplier charged Examplia VAT and the purchase is for the business's taxable activity.
- **Not recoverable (VAT-NR):** entertainment, gifts and other items the VAT Act s.30 (fictional) blocks.
- **Reverse charge (VAT-RC):** a service bought from a supplier outside Examplia. The supplier charges no VAT; the business accounts for it instead. Ask: "Is the supplier based outside Examplia?"
- **Exempt (VAT-EX):** exempt supplies such as insurance.

## The turnover levy

- It applies to every sale by a liable business, including zero-rated and exempt sales.
- Pick the levy code that matches the business's sector answer. The platform offers only the matching one.
- VAT is charged on the sale price including the levy; the platform handles this.

## Withholding on service fees

- When a customer pays and deducts withholding tax, record the receipt with the withholding code, and keep the customer's deduction certificate.
- When the business pays a supplier for services covered by the Income Tax Act s.85 (fictional), it must deduct withholding and pay it over.

## Thresholds and registration

When the platform reports that turnover is approaching the registration threshold, explain that the business may need to register and suggest it speaks to its accountant. The platform never registers anyone.

## Filing calendar

The platform supplies each return's period and due date. VAT is monthly, the levy quarterly and withholding monthly.

## Records to keep

Tax invoices issued and received, export evidence, reverse-charge supplier invoices, and withholding certificates, for the period the law requires.
